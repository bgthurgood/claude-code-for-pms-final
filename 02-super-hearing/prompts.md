# 02 · Super Hearing — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly. Last session you built a context file and met the
company.

You still do not know what actually went wrong.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.
I do well with charts, tables, graphs, diagrams, and visuals. Can you provide the information above in a more visual format?

### 2.
Do a sentiment analysis of the interviews - are any of them showing signs of being particularly disgruntled, agitated, or even optimistic

### 3.
Draw me a workflow of how the end-to-end callout process. Map the feedback from these interviews to where they occur in the workflow

### 4.
Write a user story that captures the highest priority concern we get from the interviews

### 5.
Create a backlog weighting outcomes, sentiment, redesign for the work items we are considering in version 4.3 anyway. Prioritize each work item

### 6.
Build me a matrix to help me prioritize work items based on:

* Impact on customer outcomes
* Impact on customer sentiment
* Alignment with parts of the console redesign

Turn the matrix into a more compact visual

### 7.
Build me a 4.3 release roadmap

### 8.
Looking back at the 4.3 roadmap, how should it be updated from the original plan to incorporate what we've learned from this analysis of interviews and tickets?

### 9.
It seems that between interviews and support tickets, one says that the missed pings are the priority and they other says that not getting any pings at all is the priority. What are the root causes of each of these? Are there any common root causes across both issues?

### 10.
The feedback loop seems to be the real issue. Create an initiative, epic, user story for our next sprint that focuses on addressing that root cause of missing the ping and denying it having the same effect on rating and that being exacerbated by the shift from 90 sec to 60 seconds so that when responders fall behind, they can never climb back up the rank.
